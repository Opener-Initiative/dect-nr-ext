The Spectrum Management Procedure in the FT mode RD is executed with the following goals:
- Operating carrier (channel) and subslot(s) selection by using a background scan.
- Transmitting beacons to enable other RDs in the PT mode to identify the network and initiate association with the RD in the FT mode.
- Providing and configuring radio communication parameters of the connections with associated PT RDs.

The RD in PT mode operates based on information provided by RDs in the FT mode and performs the following functions:
- Selecting RD in the FT model for association.

Deviations from the DECT-2020 NR standard:
- RDs in the PT or FT modes don't select the optimum power levels. The power levels are fixed.
- RD can operate only in one mode.
- During the background scan, RDs don't detect the same Short RD IDs and don't consider their Short RD IDs invalid.
